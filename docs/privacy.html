Shift Blocks privacy policy

Updated 8 October 2026. Publisher WJB Apps. Support: wjb127@naver.com.

Shift presets, work times, calendar entries, protected dates, pattern anchors, random seeds and work timezone stay on your device. We do not receive this schedule. No account, precise location, contacts, health or employer access is required.

Backup export and import happen only when you select a file or share destination. A backup contains your schedule; its storage and access follow the destination you choose. There is no automatic cloud synchronization. Export a backup before uninstalling or resetting; operating system backups follow device settings.

Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP-derived coarse location, advertising interactions and diagnostics to provide and measure advertising. Regional Google User Messaging Platform consent is checked before eligible ad requests. Privacy options are available when required. Refusal, offline use or unavailable ads preserve calendar functionality. Google privacy policy.

Random and repeating drafts are personal planning aids. Check your employer’s official roster and local work requirements before applying a draft. Work hours depend on your selected IANA timezone, break duration and daylight-saving rules.
